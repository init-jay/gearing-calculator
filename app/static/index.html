Shift strategy

The map runs your lap's speed trace through each setup's gearing.

  • Upshifts happen at the input Shift at RPM, like the shift trace above.
  • Downshifts wait until the lower gear pulls harder; using the same tractive-effort crossovers as the Optimal shift points table. A taller gear that is still the stronger one is simply held.
  • Corners: above of lateral load the downshift waits for the car to unwind. Upshifts still fire.
  • Shift dead time is anticipated: if a downshift cannot repay the down-and-back-up cost (2× Shift dead time) before the revs recover, then hold taller gear.
  • Never uses 1st gear.
